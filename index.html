Understanding Pneumonia

Learn about the causes, symptoms, and prevention of pneumonia to protect yourself and your loved ones

What is Pneumonia?

Lung Infection

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames air sacs in one or both lungs. The air sacs may fill with fluid or pus, causing symptoms like cough, fever, chills, and difficulty breathing.

Bacterial Pneumonia

Most common type, often caused by Streptococcus pneumoniae

Viral Pneumonia

Caused by respiratory viruses like influenza and COVID-19

Fungal Pneumonia

Less common, typically affects immunocompromised individuals

Recognize the Symptoms

Early recognition of pneumonia symptoms can lead to faster treatment and better outcomes

Fever & Chills

High fever often accompanied by shaking chills

Difficulty Breathing

Shortness of breath and rapid breathing

Persistent Cough

Cough with phlegm or blood-tinged sputum

Fatigue

Extreme tiredness and weakness

Seek Immediate Medical Attention

If you experience severe symptoms like difficulty breathing, chest pain, or high fever, contact a healthcare provider immediately.

Prevention is Key

Take these simple steps to reduce your risk of pneumonia and stay healthy

Get Vaccinated

Pneumococcal and flu vaccines can significantly reduce your risk of developing pneumonia.

Practice Good Hygiene

Wash hands frequently, use hand sanitizer, and avoid touching your face with unwashed hands.

Boost Immunity

Maintain a healthy diet, exercise regularly, get adequate sleep, and manage stress levels.

Avoid Smoking

Smoking damages lung defenses and increases susceptibility to respiratory infections.

Wear Masks

In crowded places or when around sick individuals, wearing masks can prevent transmission.

Stay Home When Sick

Rest when ill and avoid contact with others to prevent spreading infections.

Take Action Today

Don't wait for symptoms to worsen. Consult with healthcare professionals and get the care you deserve.
